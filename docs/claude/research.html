2. Lanka POS (lankabill.lk)

Positioned at small shops (mobile, computer, restaurants; also grocery, pharmacy, bakery, boutique). Sinhala / Tamil / English UI, offline mode, nationwide setup and 24/7 support, WhatsApp +94 70 620 7006.
