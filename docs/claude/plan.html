Tavrion POS — Implementation Plan

Sinhala-first offline desktop POS for a small Sri Lankan grocery shop · updated 2026-11-05

1. Vision

A fast, touch-friendly, Sinhala billing system that never stops selling when the internet drops, prints receipts, opens the cash drawer, tracks stock and customer credit, and lets the owner see sales from a phone via Supabase.

2. Benchmark: mypos.lk

myPOS ONE is a broad cloud suite (20 modules, PWA, multi-branch, finance, payroll, AI forecasting). A small grocery needs perhaps 20% of it. We copy the proven core and skip enterprise modules.

4. v1 Functional scope

Billing
Touch product grid, search (Sinhala/English), qty keypad, bill discount, hold/recall, void with PIN, returns, split payment.
Catalog
Products, categories, barcode(s), unit (pcs/kg/g), cost & sell price, reorder level, image optional.
Stock
Stock-in, adjustment, wastage, low-stock list, stock valuation.
Customers/credit
Customer list, credit sale, partial repayment, balance statement, credit limit.
Cash control
Shift open/close, opening float, drawer kick, expected vs counted cash, petty cash out.
Reports
Daily/weekly/monthly sales, item-wise, cashier-wise, credit outstanding, stock, profit.

5. Non-functional targets

  • Checkout of a 10-item bill in < 30 s; add-item latency < 100 ms.
  • Cold start < 5 s on the i7-4th-gen / 8 GB machine; RAM < 250 MB.
  • Works 100% offline; sync is background-only.
  • Zero lost sales on power cut (SQLite WAL + fsync per sale).
  • Touch targets ≥ 48 px, high contrast, usable with gloves/wet hands.
